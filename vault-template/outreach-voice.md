## Rules
*State your own non-negotiables. Julian's examples, to show the kind of thing that belongs here:*
- Always say I'm interested in the company the person works at **right now**.
- No degree, programme or graduation details in the note — people don't care, and (if relevant) don't mention one school to people from a different one.
- The field/role-area mentioned changes with the job (table below).
- Max 300 characters (LinkedIn's note limit). Short is fine.
- Ask one small thing. Never ask for a job or a referral in the first note.
- The "I saw…" line is a real fact about the company that the skill checked. Never a line from my CV dressed up as interest.
- No em dashes, no "excited to", "leverage", "delve", "synergy", "I came across your profile".

## Connection notes

Write your own templates for each type of contact. Julian's shape (adapt the brackets and specifics to yourself):

**A. Anyone at the company**
> Hi [Name], my name is [You]. I've been looking at companies in [field], since it's what I'm most passionate about, and I ran into [Company]. I saw [one specific thing]. Could you tell me a bit more about the company? Any advice would help a lot.

**B. Shared school / background** (split into "still there" vs "graduated" versions if that distinction matters for your network)
> Hey [Name], I saw we both go to [School]! My name is [You]. I'm really interested in [Company], especially since I'm looking at [field] roles. Would you be open to telling me a bit about what it's like working there?

**C. Recruiter**
> Hi [Name], my name is [You]. I'm interested in the [Role] position at [Company] and just applied. I was wondering if there's anything I could do to stand out, or anything you look for in someone at my stage? Thanks either way!

Say "just applied" only if you really did. Otherwise "and I'm about to apply".

**D. Hiring manager or team lead**
> Hi [Name], my name is [You]. I'm really interested in [field] and saw the [Role] opening on your team at [Company]. Could you tell me a bit about what the team works on day to day? Any advice would help a lot.

**E. A language variant, if you'd message some contacts in another language** — write your own version here, keeping the same structure and a familiar (not formal) register if that fits your culture.

## After they accept (under ~100 words)
Thank them, ask one question about their work or the team, then offer a short call.
> Thanks for connecting, [Name]! I'd love to hear how [their team / the company] approaches [field]. Would you have 15 minutes sometime in the next couple of weeks? Happy to work around your schedule.

## Referral ask (only after they've replied and the chat went well)
> Would you be comfortable referring me for the [Role]? I can send you the link and a two-line summary so it's easy.

Then send the referral kit: the posting link, two lines about you taken from the tailored CV, and the CV PDF.

## Nudge (7 days, no reply, once only)
> Hi [Name], just bumping this in case it got buried. No worries if you're busy!
